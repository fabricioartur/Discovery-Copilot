# Executive Summary

Northstar Retail Group is a large Latin American retail organization operating
420 stores, regional e-commerce channels, customer support teams, and a mix of
legacy and cloud systems. The company wants to reduce support effort, improve
order visibility, and standardize operational workflows across acquired brands
and country teams.

The main opportunity is an internal AI assistant for support agents and store
operations teams. The assistant would summarize order context, surface approved
policy guidance, recommend next actions, and create case notes while relying on
enterprise systems for structured data.

Northstar's current environment is fragmented. Salesforce, Zendesk, SAP ECC,
Oracle E-Business Suite, a legacy OMS, Snowflake, MuleSoft, SOAP services, SFTP
jobs, AWS, Azure, and on-premises VMware systems all play a role in the customer
service workflow. Support agents and store teams rely on manual cross-system
lookups, daily reports, and inconsistent country-specific processes.

Major pain points include stale order data, manual refund validation, duplicated
support workflows, limited loyalty visibility, and peak-season service backlogs.
These issues directly affect customer satisfaction, operating cost, and agent
productivity.

Expected outcomes include lower average handling time, better support response
consistency, faster operational exception handling, and a measurable pilot for
Brazil and Mexico within 90 days. The discovery is promising, but the next step
must narrow pilot scope, clarify security constraints, confirm integration
readiness, and baseline success metrics.
